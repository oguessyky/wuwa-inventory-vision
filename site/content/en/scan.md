# How to scan

Scanning means: you scroll your bag in the game, and it reads each tile off the screen. It never presses anything for you and never moves your character: it only looks.

## Starting

1. Double-click **`扫描 Scan`**. It waits 60 seconds for you to switch to the game.
2. Press **Alt + Tab** to switch to the game (on a Mac, click into the game window). It reads only while the game itself is in front; a browser tab of the same name does not count.
3. Open the bag.

A scan page opens in your browser (on this PC only) showing what it is reading. The bar at the top says when to act:

- **Ready (green):** scroll, or click the next thing.
- **Reading… (amber):** hold still, it is reading.

## Scrolling

- Scroll slowly, about a page at a time, keeping at least one row from the screen before; otherwise it warns that rows may have been skipped.
- Scroll through every tab it scans: **Weapons, Supplies, Development Materials, Resources**. The scan page marks the tabs not scanned yet.
- The item count at the top of the game's bag is the answer: when it read as many as that, the tab is complete.

## Clicking an item

Click a thing in the game and it reads the name and the **owned** count from the details panel on the right; that number is the game's own, the most exact. A read takes about 2.5–3 seconds: wait for the bar to say **Ready** before clicking the next.

What you clicked is remembered: the next scan needs no click for it.

## To confirm

Some tiles it cannot be sure of, and marks **to confirm**:

- A few things have identical icons (the four solvents, say, or 撞金合剂 and 划音合剂): only a click tells them apart.
- An item from a new version that the catalogue does not have yet: update the catalogue on the Update page first.

Just click those tiles in the game. The Overview says how many are waiting; clicking it shows which ones in the Inventory.

## Finishing

Press **F9** when you are done (on a Mac, **Ctrl+C** in the scan's Terminal window). Everything read is kept, and when all went well the window closes itself.

If another program has F9, it says which keys are free: see [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting).

## With a home

Each finished tab is sent to the home; if the home cannot be reached the scan runs anyway, and what it read goes with the next scan. Before scanning it takes the newest catalogue from the home, and says whether either side should update (see [Updating](/update)).
