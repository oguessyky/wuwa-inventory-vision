# The calculator

The numbers the game does not show, worked out for you: what each hit does, how near 毕业 a resonator is and what to raise, which weapon, which echo to replace first, and what a team does in a rotation. Each is worked out on the build you [scanned](/scan-resonators), so there is nothing to type in.

## How near 毕业

Pick a resonator: every skill's damage, heals and shields are listed. The box at the top compares them with their own best build: 80% of it is 小毕业, 90% is 大毕业. Each stat it rests on is a bar, marked where 小毕业 and 大毕业 fall; hover a mark for its number, or hold it on a phone.

![毕业: each stat against where 小毕业 and 大毕业 fall](screens/en/calc-grade.png)

What you change here is for trying things out: it is marked **Changed**, the resonator's box lists it under **Changed here**, ↺ puts back what was scanned, and **Back to the scan** puts back all of it. Scroll down, and the resonator stays at the top as a pill; tap it to open it.

## Another weapon?

Under the weapon, the three best for this resonator, the rest of the build as it is: a 5★ at R1, a 4★ or 3★ at R5. Switch to **Owned** to weigh only yours, at your own rank. Each shows its main stat, secondary stat and passive; **Use** puts it on.

![Another weapon: the three best and what each changes](screens/en/calc-weapons.png)

## The echoes compared

What each echo's substats add to the output, all on one scale; the weakest is marked **Replace first**. Below it, what one more substat at its top roll would add: which to keep and which to roll for, at a glance.

![The echoes compared, and what one more substat is worth](screens/en/calc-echoes.png)

## Team

Under Calculator in the column, **Team** takes up to three, each on their own build from Solo. What the others give -- outros, inherent skills, chains, weapons -- is added for you; switch off what a fight would not give. Set the order on the field and the skills each one uses, and it shows the team's damage in one rotation and each one's share.

![Team: each one's share, and the rotation](screens/en/calc-team.png)
