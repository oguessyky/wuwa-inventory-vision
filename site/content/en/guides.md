# Package guides

Every package carries its own guide: `START-HERE.html` at the top of the folder once it is unzipped (a Mac package has `START-HERE.md`). Here is the same guide, to read before downloading anything -- so you can see the steps first, then pick a package.

The Windows and home guides have a picture for every step: a number, what to do in Chinese and English, and a drawing of that step. The pictures are sketches, not screenshots of any program. The Mac guides are words only, since every step on a Mac is a line in Terminal.

## Which package

- **One Windows PC:** the full package, with the scanner and every page in one.
- **Look at it from your phone anytime:** the scanner package on the PC with the game, and a home on a NAS or an always-on box. The scanner sends each finished scan to the home, and your phone opens the home.
- **Playing on a Mac:** mac-full, or mac-scanner with a home.

Every package is on the [GitHub releases page](https://github.com/oguessyky/wuwa-inventory-vision/releases/latest). On Windows, right-click the zip → Properties → tick Unblock before unzipping.

If something is still wrong after the guide, see [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting) first; for a new version later, see [Updating](/update). A scanner only needs updating when its home is newer and the new version changed the scanner: double-click `4 更新 Update`.
