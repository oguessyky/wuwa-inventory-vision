# Safety and privacy

## It only looks at the screen

It reads the pixels on the screen, the way your own eyes do.

- No memory reads, no injection, no changes to game files, no packet capture.
- It never moves your character or fights, and never uses, crafts, sells or discards anything.
- On Windows it works only in menus like the bag; on a Mac it presses no keys at all.
- When the game is not in front, it pauses.

It is an unofficial fan-made tool, not affiliated with Kuro Games; whether to use it is your call.

## Your data

- **The player ID goes first**: it is wiped from every picture before it is saved or shown.
- **Everything stays on your own devices.** No analytics, no crash reports, no ads.
- Your phone sees it through **your own** Tailscale network, never the internet.
- A scanner sends its server only what it read and small item pictures, **never a whole frame**.

## When it goes online

Only when you press the button: a catalogue update (Kuro's wiki, GitHub data tables), setup, your own Tailscale, a scanner update (this project's GitHub).
