# WuWa Inventory Vision

**A free helper for Wuthering Waves players that knows what is in your bag.** You scroll through your in-game inventory once, and it reads every item and its count off the screen. Then it tells you what you can level with what you have, what each resonator and weapon still needs, and what to farm or buy next.

It runs on your own computer (Windows or Mac), and you can look at the results on your phone too. It only looks at the screen: it never touches the game, and never plays it for you.

![Overview: your currency and scans, what your EXP can raise, what to buy, what is short](screens/en/dashboard.png)

## What it does for you

- **No more counting by hand.** Open the bag, scroll each tab, press F9. Every item's name, rarity and count is read; for weapons also the level, refinement and who has it equipped.
- **What to farm next.** Every material against every resonator's and weapon's full build, with synthesis counted: how much you are still short, in total.
- **Plans and shopping.** Plan a resonator or weapon from where it is to where you want it; see what each shop sells, its limits, and what you are running low on.
- **On your phone.** Put it on a NAS as a "home" and open it from your phone anytime; every page is in Chinese and English.

## Which one is for you

- **One Windows PC:** take the **full** package, see [Install on Windows](/install).
- **Look at it from your phone anytime:** put a "home" on a NAS or an always-on box, and the **scanner** package on the PC with the game, see [A home on a NAS](/home).
- **Playing on a Mac:** see [Install on a Mac](/mac).

Every package is on the [GitHub releases page](https://github.com/oguessyky/wuwa-inventory-vision/releases/latest), and each has a picture guide, `START-HERE.html`.

## What you need

- Windows 10 or 11; or macOS with Kuro's own Mac client.
- About 6 GB of disk.
- An NVIDIA graphics card makes recognition much faster, but it is not needed.
- To look from your phone: a free Tailscale account; for a home, a NAS or computer with Docker.

## Is it safe?

It reads **screen pixels only**: no memory reads, no changes to the game's files, no network capture, and nothing that moves your character. Your player ID is removed from every picture before it is saved or shown. All your data stays on your own devices. See [Safety and privacy](/safety).
