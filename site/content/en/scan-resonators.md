# Scan your resonators

Besides the bag, the scan reads a resonator's own screens: the level, skill levels, weapon and echoes. What it reads goes straight into the [calculator](/calc), and the level and skill levels fill in the progress, so there is nothing to type one by one.

## How

1. Double-click **`扫描 Scan`** as usual and switch to the game.
2. Open a resonator and open each screen below in turn, waiting on each for **Ready** on the scan page.
3. For the next resonator, open **Attributes** first. The skills and weapon screens show no name: they count for the one the last Attributes screen named.
4. Press **F9** to finish.

![One resonator, each screen in turn: Attributes, Skills, Weapon, Echoes](drawings/resonator-screens.svg)

The scan itself clicks nothing; it reads only the screens you open.

## The screens

- **Attributes (属性详情):** the name, element and level, and the game's own panel: HP, ATK, DEF, Energy Regen, Crit Rate and Crit DMG.
- **Skills (技能):** each skill's level.
- **Weapon (武器):** the weapon's name, level, rank and stats.
- **Echoes (声骸):** open the echo bag and click an equipped echo: its main stats, substats, set and who wears it.

![Echoes: click an equipped echo in the echo bag](drawings/resonator-echo.svg)

Resonance chains are not read yet: they are counted from your pull history.

## After

The [calculator](/calc) starts from what was scanned and shows the game's own numbers beside its own, so a difference stands out. What you change there is marked **Changed** and goes back to the scanned value with one click.

![The readings go to the server with the scan: words and numbers only](drawings/resonator-sync.svg)

Each screen keeps its newest reading. With a server, the readings go there with the bag when the scan ends: only the words and numbers read, never a picture. Readings from several PCs meet on the server, the newer one winning.
