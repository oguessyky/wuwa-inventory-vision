# The pages

Every page is in the menu on the left; its foot switches 中文 / English. Every page fits a phone.

## Dashboard

Your currency and scans, what your EXP can raise, what to buy this round, which materials are short.

![Dashboard](screens/en/dashboard.png)

## Planner

Give a resonator or weapon a target; it counts from where it is to the target, takes off what you own, and says what is still short.

![Planner](screens/en/planner.png)

## Materials

Every material against every resonator's and weapon's full build, synthesis counted, with targets of your own.

![Materials](screens/en/materials.png)

## Resonators · Weapons

Who you have, how far each is, what each still needs; how many of each weapon, its refinement, who carries it. Open one to record where it is, and from then on only what is left counts.

![Resonators](screens/en/resonators.png)

## Inventory

Everything you own, merged across scans. Search in English, Chinese, pinyin or initials. Fix a wrong count in place.

![Inventory](screens/en/inventory.png)

## The rest

- **Cooking**: what is below the count you keep, and what you can cook now.
- **Shops**: each shop's shelf and limits; give an item a base and it shows on the dashboard when you drop below it.
- **Guess**: a daily game.
- **Update**: a new catalogue, pull history, fixes to the wiki, and which side each version changed.
