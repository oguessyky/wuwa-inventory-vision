# The pages

The column on the left lists every page: "Daily" for what you look at often, "Manage" for the rest. Every page is in Chinese and English: the 中文 / English button at the foot of the column switches.

## Overview

Where it opens: your currency and how fresh each tab's scan is, what your EXP can raise, what to buy from shops this round, which dishes and materials are short, and what your plans still need.

![Overview](screens/en/dashboard.png)

## Planner

Give a resonator or weapon a target (Lv. 90 and every skill, say): it counts from where it is now to the target, and totals what is short against your stock.

![Planner](screens/en/planner.png)

## Cooking

Dishes and potions against the amount you want to keep: what is below its target, what you can cook now, and whose special dish is whose.

## Materials

Every build material against every resonator's and weapon's full build, with synthesis counted; give any material a target of your own.

![Materials](screens/en/materials.png)

## Resonators

Who you have, how far each is, what each still needs, and which weapon each carries. It opens on the ones you **own**; "clear" shows everyone. Open one to note their level and skills, and every page counts only what is left.

![Resonators](screens/en/resonators.png)

## Weapons

Which weapons you have, how many of each, their refinement, who has each equipped, and how far your spare copies merge. It opens on the ones you own too.

## Shops

Every shop's shelf by region: limits and prices. Give what you often buy a "base", and when it runs low it shows on the Overview under "To buy this round".

## Inventory

Everything you own, merged across scans, found by Chinese, pinyin, initials or English. Each thing says where it comes from and what uses it; a wrong count can be changed right there.

![Inventory](screens/en/inventory.png)

## Guess

A daily game: guess the resonator from what the catalogue knows about them.

## Update

After a game update, fetch the new catalogue here; import your pull history; correct what the wiki has wrong; see what the scans learned; and "Data → Versions": what each version changed, on the scanner or the home.

![Update](screens/en/update.png)

## Help

How to start, what each page is for, and what to do when something does not work: the same as this site.
