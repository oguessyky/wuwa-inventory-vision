Recognition

When the catalogue's picture was not enough and a scan read an item's name from its panel, the picture from the game is kept here: later scans recognise the item by it first, without a click. On the left, the catalogue's own picture to compare with; where the catalogue is sure a picture is something else, it is marked "Maybe wrong" and listed first. Click a picture to rename it, clear its name, or say its name is right. Items drawn identically (the four solvents, say) are not here: they always need a click to tell apart.

Clear the scan data

For when a scan went wrong and you want to start over. It deletes every scan, count, icon and name the scans learned, and the faces on weapons; it keeps targets, plans, resonator progress, the pull history, settings and learned recipes. A backup goes to data/backups first.

An unofficial fan-made tool, not affiliated with Kuro Games. Wuthering Waves images and names belong to Kuro Games.
