Guess

Guess today’s resonator. Each guess tells you which facts match.

How to play
  1. Type a resonator’s name (English, Chinese, pinyin or initials) and pick one to guess.
  2. Each row shows ten facts about your guess: element, weapon, release version, birthplace, affiliation, what their signature dish does, and the weekly-boss, boss, specialty and enemy-drop materials their build uses.
  3. Normal: all ten are compared. Green = same, yellow = partly the same (the same major version; or a different dish that has an effect in common), red = different. Version also shows an arrow: is the answer earlier or later? A dish is green only when it is the very same dish.
  4. Hard: only eight are compared, without version and dish effect; green and red only. A few resonators are the same in all eight: if a row is all green and still wrong, it is the other one.
  5. Nightmare: the same eight, and no colours at all, only how many of them match. Tap a cell to colour it yourself (green, red, clear). Those are your own notes; nothing checks them. When the round ends your marks stay as they are, and a ✓ or ✗ in the corner of each says whether that mark was right; cells you left unmarked stay unmarked. If you did not get it, the answer is listed with its eight values.
  6. One resonator a day, changing at 04:00. Choose the level before your first guess of the day; it cannot be changed after. Free play is unlimited and does not count toward the streak.
